# Contributing

Gatekeeper is at the specification-draft stage (v0.1). The most useful
contributions right now are review and discussion of [SPEC.md](SPEC.md):
attacks the reconstruction protection misses, unclear rules, missing prior
art, and use cases the question language cannot express.

- **Issues are welcome** for questions, feedback and proposals.
- **Discuss before a pull request.** Please open an issue first and agree
  on the change, so nobody spends time on a PR that goes another way.
- **Sign off your commits** under the
  [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/)
  (`git commit -s`). By signing off you certify that you have the right to
  submit the contribution under the project's licence (Apache-2.0).
- **No home-made cryptography.** Proposals that need cryptographic
  primitives must use existing, audited libraries.
- Security issues: do not open a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
